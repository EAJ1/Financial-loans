What is PO Funding?+

Purchase Order (PO) Funding helps businesses fulfil purchase orders, with funding amounts from R20,000 to R250,000. The personal loan calculator above does not calculate PO Funding repayments.

Is this a real loan application?+

This is a demonstration website. You can explore loan examples, but no applications are submitted and no loans are issued through this page.

How is the repayment calculated?+

The calculator uses a sample annual interest rate of 40%, divided into monthly interest, with equal repayments over your selected term. It does not include any fees. A lender's actual quote may be different.

What should I check before borrowing?+

Review the full cost, including interest and fees, the payment schedule and the lender's terms. Consider how repayments fit alongside your essential expenses.

Can I change the loan amount or term?+

Yes. Adjust either slider in the calculator to see how your example monthly payment and total repayment change. Exploring an example creates no commitment.
